Re: Condition survey of Wharfside Court, blocks A and B

Dear Ms Nandakumar,

Thank you for instructing us to carry out the condition survey of Wharfside Court. Our team inspected both blocks between 15 and 19 September, with access to every communal area, the roof coverings of block A and eleven of the twelve flats you had arranged. The one flat we could not enter, 2B/07, was surveyed from the balcony and the corridor only.

The enclosed report records our findings element by element. In summary, the structure of both blocks is sound and we found no evidence of continuing movement. The flat roof over block B has reached the end of its life: the membrane is blistered over roughly a third of its area and has failed at two outlets, which accounts for the staining reported in flats 3B/02 and 3B/03.

We recommend that the roof is renewed before the coming winter and that the remaining items, set out in the cost schedule, are programmed over the next three years. We would be glad to prepare a specification for the roof works and to obtain competitive tenders on your behalf.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you would like to discuss the report or arrange a meeting on site.
