Annual review of the harbour trust

For the year ended 31 March 2026 · Presented to the Board on 14 July 2026

1. Purpose and scope

This review covers the operations of the trust for the financial year ended 31 March 2026. It has been prepared under the terms of the 1987 order and follows the same headings as the review of the preceding year, so that the two may be read side by side.

1.1 Basis of preparation

Figures are stated on an accruals basis and are drawn from the audited accounts. Where a figure differs from the one published last year it has been restated, and the restatement is noted.

1.1.1 Treatment of dredging costs

Dredging was previously written off in the year incurred. From this year it is capitalised over the interval between campaigns, which is five years on the main channel and three on the inner basin.

2. Traffic and revenue

Vessel movements rose by four per cent against a national decline of two. The gain is attributable almost entirely to the aggregates berth, which operated for eleven months against seven in the prior year.

2.1 Charges

Harbour dues were held flat for a third year. The Board will need to decide before the autumn whether that can continue, given the dredging programme set out below.

3. Capital programme

Three schemes were carried during the year. The quay wall survey completed on time. The pontoon replacement completed six weeks late at a cost overrun of nine per cent. The lock gate refurbishment has been deferred to 2027. 3

3.1 Deferred works

Deferral of the lock gates is the single largest open risk. The gates were last refurbished in 1998 against a design life of twenty-five years, and the most recent inspection recorded significant wastage on the lower hinge.

4. Governance

The Board met seven times. Two members reached the end of their term and were not reappointed; recruitment for both seats is under way and is expected to conclude before the November meeting.

4.1 Register of interests

The register was reviewed in January. One interest was declared in relation to the aggregates contract and the member concerned withdrew from that item.
