1. Services

1. The Supplier shall provide the bookkeeping, payroll preparation and management reporting services described in Schedule A (the "Services".)

  • 2. The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with good industry practice.
  • 3. The Client shall give the Supplier timely access to records, staff and premises as reasonably required.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

4. The Client shall pay the monthly fee of £1,850 plus VAT at the prevailing rate, invoiced on the first working day of each month.2

5. Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Late sums carry interest at 4% above the Bank of England base rate.

  • 6. Expenses are recharged at cost where approved in writing in advance.

3. CONDITIONS TO BE SATISFIED BEFORE THE START DATE

The Services begin on 1 April 2019 once each of the following has been confirmed by the Client:

  • Signed copy of this agreement returned to the Supplier
  • Anti-money-laundering identity checks completed for all directors
  • Read-only access granted to the Client's accounting system
  • Opening balances supplied for the year ended 31 December 2018
  • Payroll authority letter received from the Client's bank

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA

7. Each party shall keep the other’s confidential information secret and use it only for the purposes of this agreement.

8. The Supplier shall process personal data only on the Client’s documented instructions and in line with data protection law.33

9. On termination the Supplier shall return or delete the Client's records within 30 days, unless the law requires it to keep them.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION

10. This agreement runs for an initial term of twelve months and then continues until either party gives three months’ written notice.

  • 11. Either party may end it at once by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not put right within 14 days of notice.
  • 12. This agreement is governed by the law of Scotland, and the Scottish courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
