CHAPTER 7

How Things Cool Down

A cup of tea left on the desk loses heat quickly at first, then ever more slowly. This page sets out the pattern,
gives the law behind it and shows how each quantity enters. Revised 11 March 2023 for the Taunton and
Stockport editions.

The law

The rate of cooling is proportional to the gap between the body and its surroundings.

T(t) = T_e + (T_0 - T_e)e^{-kt}

Here T_e is room temperature and k the cooling constant.

Half-life of the gap

The gap halves every 0.69/k minutes, whatever its starting size.

t_{1/2} = \ln 2 / k

For k = 0.05 per minute, t_{1/2} is about 14 minutes.
