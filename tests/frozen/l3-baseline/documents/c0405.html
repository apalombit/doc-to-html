SUMMER SOUPS

Chilled sorrel soup with new potatoes

By Nadia Adeyemi · Indigo Table · Updated July 28, 2019

PREP

COOK
15 min25 min
CHILLSERVES
2 hours41

Ingredients

FOR THE SOUP

  • 1 lb (450 g) new potatoes, scrubbed and halved
  • 4 cups (1 liter) vegetable stock
  • 8 oz (225 g) fresh sorrel, stems removed
  • 1 small white onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) sour cream, plus extra to serve
  • Salt and white pepper

TO SERVE

  • Snipped chives
  • 4 soft-boiled eggs, halved
  • Lemon wedges

Method

  • 1. Simmer the potatoes in the stock until tender, about 12 minutes. Lift them out with a slotted spoon and set aside to cool.
  • 2. Soften the onion in the butter over low heat for 6 minutes. Add the sorrel and stir until it turns olive green and collapses.
Per serving:
210 calories · vitamin B6 14% · iron 9%
