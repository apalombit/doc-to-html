ASHBURY COMMUNITY ARTS FESTIVAL

Lanterns on
the River

Saturday 14 June · 7 – 11 pm
Ferry Wharf and the Old Towpath, Ashbury
MUSIC

Brass bands on the wharf, a folk stage under the bridge and a late DJ set by the boathouse.

FOOD

Twenty local stalls, from wood-fired flatbreads to Sri Lankan hoppers and hot cider.

WORKSHOPS

Make your own paper lantern from 4 pm in the library hall. All materials provided.

Free entry · Family friendly
