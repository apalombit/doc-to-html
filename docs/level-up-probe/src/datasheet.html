AQ-7 Indoor Air Quality Sensor

VERIDIAN

The AQ-7 measures particulate matter, CO₂, temperature and humidity in one compact wall-mounted unit, and reports over Modbus or BACnet for direct integration with building management systems.

Key features

  • Laser PM1.0, PM2.5 and PM10 sensing
  • NDIR CO₂ with automatic baseline correction
  • Three-colour status ring visible at 10 m
  • Field-replaceable sensor cartridge
  • Tool-free mounting on a standard back box
  • Five-year warranty
