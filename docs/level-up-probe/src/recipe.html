Roasted Squash & Farro Salad

with brown butter, sage and toasted hazelnuts
Prep 20 minCook 35 minServes 4Vegetarian

Ingredients

For the salad

  • 1 small butternut squash (about 900 g)
  • 200 g pearled farro
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 60 g hazelnuts, roughly chopped
  • 100 g baby spinach
  • 80 g feta, crumbled

For the dressing

  • 40 g unsalted butter
  • 8 sage leaves
  • 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
  • 1 tsp maple syrup

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Peel and seed the squash, cut it into 2 cm cubes, toss with the oil and a good pinch of salt, and roast for 30–35 minutes until caramelised at the edges.
  2. Meanwhile, simmer the farro in salted water for 25 minutes, until tender but still chewy. Drain well and spread on a tray to steam dry.
  3. Toast the hazelnuts in a dry pan over a medium heat for 3–4 minutes, shaking often.
  4. For the dressing, melt the butter with the sage leaves and cook until the butter smells nutty and turns golden. Off the heat, whisk in the vinegar and maple syrup.
  5. Toss the warm farro and squash with the spinach and most of the dressing. Scatter over the feta and hazelnuts, drizzle with the rest of the dressing and serve warm.
Cook's tip — The farro can be cooked a day ahead; warm it through in the roasting tin for the last five minutes.
Per serving: 520 kcal · 24 g fat · 9 g saturates · 61 g carbohydrate · 14 g protein · 1.1 g salt
