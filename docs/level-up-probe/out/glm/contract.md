SERVICE AGREEMENT
Agreement no. SA-2026-0457 · dated 3 October 2026

This Agreement is made between Calder Valley Veterinary Group LLP, of 12 Bridge Street, Hebden Bridge HX7 8EX (the "Client"), and Northlight Systems Ltd, a company registered in England under number 09912345, of 7 Wharf Road, Sowerby Bridge HX6 2AG (the "Provider").

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Services" means the managed IT support described in Schedule 1, including remote monitoring, helpdesk and on-site attendance.
1.2 "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.
1.3 "Response Time" means the period between the Client logging a fault and the Provider beginning work on it.

2. TERM
2.1 This Agreement starts on 1 November 2026 and continues for an initial period of 24 months, after which it renews for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives at least 90 days' written notice.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 The Client shall pay a monthly fee of £1,850 plus VAT, invoiced in advance on the first Business Day of each month.
3.2 The Provider may charge additionally for:
   (a) on-site attendance outside 08:00 – 18:00 on Business Days, at £95 per hour; and
   (b) hardware and licences, at cost plus 8%, only with the Client’s prior written approval.
3.3 Invoices are payable within 30 days. Late payments carry interest at 4% a year above the Bank of England base rate.
