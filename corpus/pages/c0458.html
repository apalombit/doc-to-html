Ingredients

Rolled oats (52%), walnuts (12%), honey (9%), sunflower oil, brown sugar, sunflower seeds, dried apple (3%), cinnamon, sea salt.

Allergy advice: for allergens, including cereals containing gluten, see ingredients in bold. Contains oats and walnuts. May contain other nuts, milk and sesame.

Serving ideas

  1. 1.Pour 40 g into a bowl.
  2. 2.Add 125 ml cold milk or plain yoghurt.
  3. 3.Top with sliced fruit and eat within the hour.

Storage

  1. 1.Keep cool and dry, out of sunlight.
  2. 2.Reseal the bag after opening.
  3. 3.Eat within 6 weeks of opening.
