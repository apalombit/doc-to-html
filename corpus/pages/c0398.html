Quince and Almond Tart

From the Whitlow Kitchen Notebook, autumn series
Serves six  ·  Prep 40 minutes  ·  Bake 45 minutes  ·  Cost about £9.80

Method

Peel and quarter the quinces, remove the cores, and poach them gently in a syrup of 100 g sugar, the vanilla pod and 500 ml water until they turn rose pink and tender, about forty minutes.

While they cook, rub 100 g of the butter into the flour, bind with one egg yolk and a spoonful of cold water, and chill the dough wrapped in cloth for half an hour.

Roll the pastry thin and line a 24 cm tin, then blind bake it for fifteen minutes at 180 °C.

To finish

Beat the remaining butter and sugar until pale, then add the two eggs and the ground almonds and spread this frangipane across the pastry base.

Drain the quince, slice each quarter and fan the slices over the filling. Bake for about thirty minutes, until the top is set and golden at the edges.

Brush with a little reduced poaching syrup while warm. Serve in the afternoon with thick cream.
