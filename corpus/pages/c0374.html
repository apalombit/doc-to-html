Cover story

Twenty years of making things that last

our first workshop, as it was

This month Lark & Vale turns twenty. What began in 2004 as two benches in a leaking shed outside Dundee is now a team of thirty-one, a second site in Newry and a waiting list that Freya Stirling cheerfully admits she has stopped trying to shorten.

"We never meant to be big," she says. "We meant to make a table that a family could eat at for fifty years, and then to make the next one properly too." That habit has carried through every product, from the oak dining range to the small wooden toys that now fill the Newry showroom.

To mark the anniversary, every order placed before 31 October includes a hand-finished oak board, engraved with the year the customer first ordered from us, if they did.

People

Meet the new head of finishing

Declan Gallagher joined us in August from a boatyard on Strangford Lough, where he spent eleven years oiling, waxing and rescuing things other people had given up on. He now runs our finishing room, and says the secret is patience and a very good rag.

"Wood tells you when it is ready," he says. "The skill is mostly in listening." His first project is a revised care guide, which will be posted to every customer and sent with each new delivery.

Tips

Caring for oak through the heating season

Dry air is the enemy of solid timber. Keep furniture at least a metre from radiators, wipe up spills at once, and refresh the oil twice a year with a thin coat and a soft cloth. Do not use wax and oil together.
