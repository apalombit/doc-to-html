Overview

Nuthatch Freight closed fiscal 2022 with the strongest results in its history. Revenue grew 9.6 percent to $182.4 million, led by container drayage out of the port of Duluth, Minnesota, and by the second full year of the Clover Valley terminal in Rochester, Minnesota. Demand stayed firm through the winter quarter, when ice on the lake normally slows volumes, and the new rail connection at Duluth kept transit times inside the targets we set with customers in July 2021.1

Results of operations

Operating income rose to $21.7 million from $18.0 million, and the operating margin widened from 10.8 percent to 11.9 percent. Fuel remained the largest source of pressure: diesel cost us $14.2 million, or 7.8 percent of revenue, against 6.1 percent a year earlier. Fuel surcharges recovered about 71 percent of that increase.2 Wages and benefits grew 6.4 percent as we hired 112 drivers and technicians, and the share of loads moved by company drivers reached 78 percent.

Net income was $15.3 million. Capital spending totaled $19.6 million, mainly 46 replacement tractors and the expansion of the Rochester yard. We ended the year with $24.1 million in cash and no borrowings against the revolving facility.

Highlights of the year

  • Container volume rose 14 percent, to 118,000 twenty-foot equivalent units.
  • On-time delivery reached 96.2 percent, the highest rate since records began in 2014.
  • Safety: recordable incidents fell to 1.9 per 200,000 hours worked.
  • The Rochester yard added 62 trailer positions and a covered inspection bay.
  • Luis Le joined as Vice President of Operations in March 2022.

1 Transit-time targets are measured door to door, from gate-in at origin to gate-out at the consignee.

2 Surcharge recovery is the surcharge billed divided by the estimated fuel cost increase over fiscal 2021.
