Open Day and Plot Sign-up
Saturday 1 June 2024, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.
Sundial Lane site, Shrewsbury
Welcome
Our 62 plots have fed Shrewsbury families since 1946. Whether you have never held a trowel or have grown leeks for thirty years, come and see what a plot involves before you take one on.
How to get a plot
1
Visit. Walk the site with a committee member. Oliver Treloar, the site secretary, leads tours on the hour.
2
Choose. Half plots (5 rods) cost £18 a year; full plots (10 rods) cost £32. Water is included.
3
Sign. Complete the tenancy form with Stuart Kowalski at the gate hut and pay your first year by card or cash.
On the day
  • Seed swap and cutting table by the main gate
  • Children's scarecrow workshop at 11.30
  • Honey, jam and chutney stall, with tea and cake in the hut
  • Bring boots, as the paths are muddy after rain
  • Dogs on leads, please; compost bins are open to all
