About this survey

Rowan Vale Group runs this questionnaire every year so that managers can see how working life feels across our Shrewsbury and Taunton offices.

It takes about fifteen minutes. Please answer every section if you can.

Staff Wellbeing Questionnaire

Thank you for taking part. Your answers help us decide where to put time and money in the coming year, from the layout of the offices to the hours we expect people to keep. There are no right or wrong answers; we would rather hear how things really are than how they ought to be. Results will be shared with all staff in April 2019 and the same questions will be asked again a year from now so that we can see whether anything has changed.
