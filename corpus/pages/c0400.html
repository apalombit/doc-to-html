Carlos Holloway, who has managed the Lantern Foods Co-op in Rochester, MN since 2012, named three conditions that held in every successful store he visited:

  1. 1.Local sourcing that members can see
    • at least a third of produce from farms within 60 miles
    • a published list of growers at the door
  2. 2.A member discount that shows on the receipt
  3. 3.A board that publishes its minutes
    • monthly, within ten days of the meeting
