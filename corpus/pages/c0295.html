3. Progress this fortnight

  • Steel: the last of the 14 trusses was lifted on 7 August; Finlay Burrows reports the Colchester fabricator is now clear of the programme.
  • Purlins and bracing: 70% fixed on the north slope, and the east gable is ready for decking.
  • Listed-building consent: the variation for the new gutter profile was approved on 5 August by the Salisbury conservation officer.
  • Traders: the hall stayed open on all but two days, and no stall was moved more than once.

4. Risks and issues

  • New: the roof membrane lead time has moved from six weeks to nine, which puts the 28 November finish at risk unless the order is placed by 22 August.
  • Open: asbestos survey of the loading bay soffit is outstanding; a result is promised by 18 August.
  • Decision needed: Stuart Sinclair, as sponsor, is asked to approve an early order of the membrane at a cost of £31,200.
