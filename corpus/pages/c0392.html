Baking Your First Loaf

Program 1 · Basic White

This walk-through bakes a 2 lb white loaf on the Basic program. Read Important Safeguards on page 3 before first use, and wash the bread pan and kneading paddle in warm soapy water.

At a glance

Program: 1 Basic

Crust: Medium

Loaf size: 2 lb

Total time: 3 h 20 min

Keep warm: 60 min

Delay timer: up to 13 h

Power: 120 V ~ 60 Hz

Rating: 650 W

Ingredients, 2 lb loaf

IngredientAmount
Water, lukewarm (80 °F)1¼ cups
Vegetable oil2 tbsp
Bread flour4 cups
Nonfat dry milk2 tbsp
Sugar2 tbsp
Salt1½ tsp
Active dry yeast2¼ tsp

Steps

1

Remove the bread pan. Turn the pan counterclockwise and lift it out by the handle. Fit the kneading paddle onto the drive shaft, flat side down.

2

Add the liquids first. Pour the water and oil into the pan. Always add ingredients in the order listed above.

3

Cover with the dry ingredients. Spoon the flour over the water so it is completely covered, then place the milk powder, sugar and salt in separate corners.

4

Make a well for the yeast. With a finger, make a shallow hollow in the top of the flour and pour the yeast into it. The yeast must not touch the water or the salt until mixing starts.

5

Lock the pan in place. Lower the pan into the baking chamber and turn it clockwise until it clicks. Close the lid and plug in the machine.

6

Choose the settings. Press MENU until the display shows 1, then SIZE for 2 lb and CRUST for Medium. The display reads 1  3:20.

7

Press START/STOP. The machine beeps once and kneading begins. A second beep at 2:40 is the add-in signal for raisins or nuts. Do not open the lid during rising or baking.

8

Remove the loaf. When the machine beeps 10 times, press and hold START/STOP for 3 seconds. Wearing oven mitts, turn the pan upside down over a wire rack and shake the loaf out. Let it cool for 20 minutes before slicing.

Caution

The bread pan, the paddle and the inside of the lid are very hot after baking. Never put your hand into the baking chamber while the unit is plugged in. If the paddle stays in the loaf, let it cool before removing it with the hook supplied.

A note from our test kitchen

Measure flour by spooning it into the cup and leveling it off. Packed flour can add a quarter cup or more and gives a short, dense loaf.

Happy baking! — Josie
