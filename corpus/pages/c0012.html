Notes and data protection
Permits are valid only for the vehicle and zone named above and must be displayed on the inside of the windscreen. Lost or stolen permits can be replaced for £15.00 on production of a crime reference number. If you move house or change vehicle you must tell Parking Services within 14 days, and the permit will be reissued without a further fee if the new address is in the same zone.
Marlowe Borough Council will use the information on this form to process your application, prevent fraud and enforce parking restrictions. It will be kept for six years after the permit expires and will not be shared except where the law requires. You may ask to see or correct it by writing to the Data Protection Officer at the address below. Applications take up to 10 working days to process.
