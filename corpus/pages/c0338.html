Subject:Revised travel and expenses limits, 2026/27

Date: 24 October 2026      Ref: FIN/26/117

Following the Board's review on 14 October, the limits below take effect for all journeys booked on or after 1 November 2026. They replace the figures circulated in April.

The main changes are as follows:

  • Standard-class rail travel remains the default; first class needs prior written approval from a Director.
  • The nightly hotel limit is raised to £145 in London and £110 elsewhere in the United Kingdom.
  • The daily subsistence allowance becomes £32.50, of which no more than £12.00 may be claimed for breakfast.
  • Mileage for private cars is reimbursed at £0.45 per mile for the first 10,000 miles, and £0.25 thereafter.
  • Claims over £250 must be supported by itemised receipts and submitted within 30 days.

We estimate that the revised limits will add roughly £18,400 to the annual travel budget, set against projected savings of £21,900 from the new booking platform. The net effect is therefore a modest saving of £3,500.

Please brief your teams before the end of the month. Queries should go to the Finance Office, extension 4410, and not to the Company Secretary.

With thanks,
