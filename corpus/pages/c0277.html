Merlin Cold Storage, Inc.

TEMPERATURE-CONTROLLED LOGISTICS

Tulsa, OK  |  Macon, GA

Quarterly Operating Review

First quarter of 2023

Prepared by:  Raj Nguyen, Director of Operations

Reviewed by:  Kayla Vasquez, Chief Financial Officer

Approved by:  Ashley Gutierrez, Chief Executive Officer

Date:  April 23, 2023

Period covered:  January 1 to March 31, 2023

1.  Executive summary

Throughput at the Tulsa, OK facility rose 6.4% over the fourth quarter of 2022, to 48,200 pallet movements, while the Macon, GA facility held steady at 31,750 movements.1 Combined revenue for the quarter was $14.8 million, against $13.9 million in the prior quarter.2 Operating margin improved from 11.2% to 12.6%.

The new evaporator units at Tulsa cut electricity use to 3.9 kWh per 1,000 ft3 of chilled space, and the two sites together emitted 1,240 metric tons of CO2, down 8% on the quarter.3 Occupancy of the 84,000 ft2 chilled floor area averaged 91%.

Outbound deliveries met the agreed window on 97.3% of 6,410 loads, the best result since the Macon site opened. Claims for product temperature excursions fell to 4 in the quarter from 11, and none exceeded $5,000.4 Labor hours per 100 pallets moved fell from 14.2 to 13.1 after the dock scheduling change in February.

2.  Capital projects

Phase 2 of the Macon racking extension added 2,400 pallet positions and was commissioned on March 6, two weeks ahead of plan. Spending to date is $3.1 million of the $3.6 million approved by the Board; the balance covers the fire-suppression inspection and the final sign-off.

1 A pallet movement counts each inbound or outbound pallet once.

2 Revenue is shown before intercompany adjustments.

3 Emissions are estimated from metered electricity and refrigerant top-ups.

4 Excursions are readings above 2 °C for more than 30 minutes.
