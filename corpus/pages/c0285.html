The Nuthatch retrofit programme

In 2018/19 Cypress Housing Group completed energy upgrades to 1,284 homes, 94 per cent of those planned for the year. Insulation, new heating controls and double glazing lifted the average energy rating of treated homes by nine points. “Residents notice the difference in their bills before they notice it in the walls,” said Sophie Chowdhury, Director of Asset Management, reviewing the programme on 14 February 2019.
