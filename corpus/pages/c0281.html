TOPAZ PROPERTY SURVEYS    |    Lancaster

Roof and chimneys

Section 5 of the building inspection report  ·  27 Teasel Lane, Dundee  ·  Inspected 8 May 2022

  1. 1.Main roof. Pitched, covered in concrete interlocking tiles, probably original to the house. On the north slope several tiles have slipped and three are cracked; two are missing above the rear bedroom.1
  2. 2.Ridge. The mortar bedding is cracked along about a third of its length. It should be re-bedded within twelve months.
  3. 3.Chimney. A brick stack on the party wall. The flaunching is cracked, one pot is leaning, and the lead flashing has lifted on the windward side.2
  4. 4.Gutters. The cast-iron gutters at the rear are corroded, and a downpipe joint is leaking onto the wall below.
  1. 5.Loft. Viewed from the hatch only, as the floor is part boarded. Mineral wool insulation about 100 mm deep, well below the 270 mm now advised.3
  2. 6.Roof timbers. No sagging seen. No sign of active beetle. Moisture in the rafters read 14%, which is acceptable.
  3. 7.Action. Ask a roofer to repair items 1 to 4 within three months and to quote for re-bedding the ridge at the same visit.

Condition rating 3 – repairs needed soon.

1 no repair needed  ·  2 normal upkeep  ·  3 repair soon

1 Tiles were counted from the garden with binoculars. The front slope was not reached by ladder.

2 Photographs 14 and 15 in the appendix show the flashing and the pot.

3 The depth is the mean of three readings taken by Imogen Kaur, with Ruth Lindsay and Amelia Burrows present.
