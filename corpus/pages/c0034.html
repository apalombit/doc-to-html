Instructions by line

Lines 1 to 9

Enter every amount shown on your wage statements. Include tips you did not report to your employer. Line 5 is rent received less costs of repairs, taxes and interest.

If line 9 is more than $100,000, you cannot use this form. Use the long form AR-40 instead.

Lines 10 to 17

Educator expenses may not exceed $300 for each qualified person. Student loan interest is limited to $2,500 for the year.

Keep the receipts for any deduction you claim for five years.

Lines 18 to 21

Joint filers claim a standard deduction of $14,600. The qualified business deduction on line 19 is 20% of net business income on line 4, rounded down.

Lines 22 to 28

Find your taxable income in the rate table and read across to your filing status. Credits cannot reduce your tax below zero.

Lines 29 to 38

Attach a copy of each wage statement that shows tax withheld. If you owe, pay by the due date shown above to avoid the penalty on line 38.

For a refund by direct deposit, complete boxes I, J and K.

Where to file

Atlas State Department of Revenue, Individual Returns Unit, Box 2208, Fort Collins, CO 80522.
