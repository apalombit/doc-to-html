Survey

Scheme: Winter Thrush Transect

Transects: 20–22 (Cairn), 14–16 (Shrewsbury)

Visits: monthly, four per winter

Lead observer: Zara Doherty

Data to: 17 November 2024

Figure 2. Fieldfare numbers at Cairn Moor were consistently about twice those on the Shrewsbury farmland, and both sites rose and fell together, peaking in 2022/23 (21.7 and 12.4 birds per transect) after the poor 2021/22 winter.1 Counts are the mean of each transect’s four visits, averaged over transects. Source: Cairn Fieldfare Project, field returns collated by Stuart Burrows and Megan Lindsay.
