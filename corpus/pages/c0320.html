1 · The Notice

1.1This notice is given by Pellham Estates Management Limited, on behalf of Albion Wharf Residents’ Freehold Limited (“the Landlord”), to each leaseholder of Albion Wharf, 14–22 Riverside Walk, Bristol BS1 6XQ (“the Building”). It is the first stage of consultation required before the Landlord may recover the cost of qualifying works through the service charge in excess of £250 for any one flat.

1.2The Landlord intends to carry out the works described in section 2. No contract has yet been entered into, and no estimate has yet been obtained.

2 · Description of the Proposed Works

2.1The works comprise (a) stripping and renewal of the flat roof coverings to Blocks A and C, including new insulation, outlets and lead flashings to the parapets; (b) repointing of the brick elevations facing Riverside Walk and the inner courtyard, with replacement of spalled bricks and cracked stone sills; (c) overhaul and redecoration of the steel balconies to the south elevation; and (d) the scaffolding, access and professional fees necessary for (a) to (c).

2.2The works will be supervised by a chartered building surveyor appointed by the Landlord, whose fees will form part of the cost of the works.

3 · Reasons for the Works

3.1The Landlord considers the works necessary because the condition survey of the Building¹ found the existing roof coverings at the end of their serviceable life, with water ingress recorded in four top-floor flats during the winter of 2025, and identified open joints and frost damage to the brickwork that will worsen if left untreated. Carrying out the roof and façade repairs together allows one scaffold to serve both, which the surveyor estimates will reduce the access costs by about a third.

4 · Observations and Nominations

4.1You are invited to make written observations in relation to the proposed works by sending them to the address in the margin. Observations must be received within the relevant period of 30 days beginning with the date of this notice, which ends on 18 November 2026².

4.2You may also propose, within the same period, the name of a person from whom the Landlord should try to obtain an estimate for the works³. Where a single nomination is received, the Landlord will try to obtain an estimate from that person; where several are received, from the person who received the most nominations.

5 · What Happens Next

5.1After the relevant period has closed the Landlord will obtain estimates and send every leaseholder a statement setting out at least two of them, a summary of any observations received and the Landlord’s response. You will then have a further 30 days to comment before any contract is placed.
