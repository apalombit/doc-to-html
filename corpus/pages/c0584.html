Read this as

  • Ayr was counted on 24 of the 41 survey days, led by Yusuf Rahman, with the same transects as 2026.
  • Burrow occupancy is a count of signs, not of birds; the index moves with the weather in May.
  • The heron fall is the first of more than 10% in six seasons.
  • Counts are the mean of three visits to each transect, taken a week apart.

Still open

  • Callum Caldwell found two nests lost to the March storm and three with no cause given.
  • Two observers covered Colchester from the hide and the river wall, so a nest missed in the reeds is possible.
  • Disturbance on the estuary path rose after the new car park opened in April.
  • A second visit in July would show whether the failed pairs re-laid elsewhere.
  • Isla Hughes will ask the council for the path counter data before the board meets.
