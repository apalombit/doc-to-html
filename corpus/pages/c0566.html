Amber Ridge Woodshop presents
Build Your Own Timber Bench
A one-day beginners' workshop in Boise, ID
What you will learn
  • Reading a board and choosing sound lumber
  • Cutting and fitting a mortise and tenon joint
  • Hand planing, scraping and sanding
  • Finishing with oil and wax
  • Taking home a bench you built yourself
