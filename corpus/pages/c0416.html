Goldcrest Farm blueberry skillet cake

Tested by Tyler Yamamoto, Ithaca, NY, on 27 July 2022

Prep20 min
Bake35 min at 350°F
Serves8
Pan10-inch cast iron

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups (190 g)all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup (150 g)granulated sugar
  • 1½ tspbaking powder
  • ½ tspfine salt
  • ½ cup (115 g)unsalted butter, melted, plus 1 tbsp for the pan
  • 2 largeeggs, at room temperature
  • ⅔ cup (160 ml)whole milk
  • 2 cups (300 g)wild blueberries1
  • 1 tbspturbinado sugar, for the top

Method

  1. 1.Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C)2 and put the buttered skillet inside while you mix.
  2. 2.Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl.
  3. 3.Beat the eggs with the melted butter and milk, pour into the dry mixture and fold until just combined; a few streaks of flour are fine.
  4. 4.Fold in two thirds of the blueberries, pour the batter into the hot skillet and scatter the rest on top.
  5. 5.Sprinkle with turbinado sugar and bake for 35 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
  6. 6.Rest for 15 minutes before cutting into eight wedges.

1 Small wild berries from a grower near Bangor, ME, were used. Frozen ones go in unthawed; add 5 minutes.

2 The oven ran 15°F hot in the test kitchen, so the temperature was checked with a separate thermometer.
