Residents’ Forum  ·  Minutes of the meeting held on 21 February 2019

Ref. RF/2019/02

Page 2 of 3

Venue: Meeting Room 2, Zephyr House, Swindon.   Time: 18:30 to 20:05.   Chair: Sophie Ashworth.   Minutes: Ewan Sinclair.

Present: Sophie Ashworth (Head of Neighbourhoods), Kwame Nwosu (Tenants’ representative, Truro), Ewan Sinclair (Governance Officer) and eleven residents.

Items 4 and 5

  1. 4.1Sophie Ashworth presented the repairs report for January.
    • 38 jobs were open, down from 61 in December.
    • All 14 emergency jobs were completed within 24 hours.
    • Damp and mould cases:
      • nine reported, six inspected so far;
      • three more in the Truro blocks awaiting a surveyor.
  2. 4.2Kwame Nwosu asked that the Truro blocks be inspected before the end of March, as residents had waited since November. Sophie Ashworth agreed.
  3. 4.3The Forum noted the revised contractor list and asked for contact numbers to be reissued to every household.
    • Out-of-hours line to be printed in bold.
    • Residents without e-mail to receive a paper copy.
  4. 5.1Any other business: the heating survey for floors 1 to 4 was approved for circulation. The Chair thanked those present.
