Subject
Moving the Kendal evening sort to Barnsley

1Summary

Kendal’s evening sort has run at or above 94% of belt capacity since October.1 We propose moving it to Barnsley from the week beginning 14 June, keeping the morning sort and the van fleet where they are. This note sets out what moves, what stays, and what we need from you by 12 March so that the lease variation can be signed in good time.

2What moves

  • The evening sort, about 4,200 parcels a night, Monday to Saturday
  • Three belt operators and one shift lead, by agreement with each
  • The returns cage, which Barnsley already has room for2
  • Nothing else: vans, drivers and the morning sort stay at Kendal

1 Belt utilisation from the depot scanner logs, October 2026 to January 2027, weekday evenings.

2 Floor survey by Declan Fairclough, 9 February 2027; the cage needs 38 square metres.
