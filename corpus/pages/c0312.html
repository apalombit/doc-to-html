Hartwell Lodge Trust  and  Fenwick & Orme Conservation Ltd

AGREEMENT FOR CONSERVATION SERVICES

for the repair of the east range roof and plasterwork, Hartwell Lodge, Shropshire

Agreement no. HLT/2026/014 Dated 24 October 2026 Commencement 2 November 2026

Parties

  1. (1)HARTWELL LODGE TRUST, a charity registered in England and Wales under number 1172604, whose principal office is at Hartwell Lodge, Ludlow Road, Church Stretton SY6 7BQ (the “Trust”); and
  2. (2)FENWICK & ORME CONSERVATION LIMITED, incorporated in England and Wales with company number 08816243, whose registered office is at Unit 4, Severn Side Works, Shrewsbury SY1 2LT (the “Contractor”).

Background

(A)  The Trust owns and maintains Hartwell Lodge, a Grade II* listed house, and wishes to have the lead roof coverings and the lime plaster ceilings of its east range repaired in accordance with the schedule of works prepared by its surveyor and dated 11 September 2026 (the “Schedule”).

(B)  Listed building consent for the works was granted by Shropshire Council on 3 October 2026 under reference 26/03918/LBC1, subject to the conditions set out in that consent, one of which is reproduced in clause 4.3 below.

Agreed terms

  1. 1.Interpretation. In this Agreement the following definitions apply:
    1. 1.1“Works” means the works described in the Schedule, together with any variation instructed in writing under clause 6;
    2. 1.2“Site” means the east range of Hartwell Lodge, its roof space, the scaffold zone shown hatched on drawing HL-SK-07 and the agreed access route from the stable yard;
    3. 1.3“Working Day” means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.
  2. 2.Term. This Agreement starts on 2 November 2026 and, unless terminated earlier under clause 9, continues until the Works are certified complete. The Contractor shall use reasonable endeavours to reach practical completion on or before 30 April 2027.
  3. 3.Contract sum and payment.
    1. 3.1The Trust shall pay the Contractor the sum of £148,620.00 exclusive of VAT (the “Contract Sum”), adjusted only for variations agreed under clause 6.
    2. 3.2The Contractor shall submit an application for payment on the last Working Day of each month, valuing the Works properly executed. The Trust shall pay each certified sum within 30 days of the application2.
    3. 3.3A retention of 3 per cent shall be deducted from each payment. One half shall be released on practical completion and the balance twelve months afterwards, on the making good of defects.
  4. 4.Standard of work.
    1. 4.1The Contractor shall carry out the Works with the skill and care of a competent conservation contractor experienced in works to listed buildings of similar age and character.
    2. 4.2All materials shall match the existing in substance and appearance. In particular:
      1. (a)replacement lead shall be cast sheet of Code 7 or heavier, laid in bays not exceeding the lengths stated in the Schedule;
      2. (b)plaster shall be a non-hydraulic lime putty mix, haired, applied in three coats on riven oak lath; and
      3. (c)no cement, gypsum or synthetic resin shall be used anywhere in the Works without the prior written approval of the Trust’s surveyor.
    3. 4.3The Contractor acknowledges condition 3 of the listed building consent, which reads:

      “No historic plasterwork shall be removed from the building until a written record and photographic survey of it has been submitted to and approved in writing by the Local Planning Authority, and any fragments of decorative cornice so removed shall be retained on site for reinstatement.”

      Shropshire Council, consent 26/03918/LBC, condition 3
  5. 5.Insurance. The Contractor shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than £5,000,000 for any one event and contractor’s all-risks cover for the full reinstatement value of the Works, and shall produce evidence of both on request3.
  6. 6.Variations. No variation to the Works shall be valid unless it is instructed in writing by the Trust’s surveyor. The Contractor shall within five Working Days of an instruction give a written estimate of its cost and of any effect on the completion date.
  7. 7.Access and occupation. The house remains open to visitors from Wednesday to Sunday. The Contractor shall keep the visitor route clear, shall not work in the Great Parlour between 10.00 and 16.00 on opening days, and shall secure the Site at the end of each working day.
  8. 8.Confidentiality. Each party shall keep confidential the terms of this Agreement and any information concerning the other’s business, save as required by law or by the Charity Commission.
  9. 9.Termination.
    1. 9.1Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach which, if remediable, is not remedied within 14 days of a notice requiring it.
    2. 9.2On termination the Trust shall pay for the Works properly executed up to the date of termination, less the retention and any sum the Trust is entitled to deduct.
  10. 10.Governing law. This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be governed by the law of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.

Signed by the parties on the date first written above.
