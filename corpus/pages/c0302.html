Notice to tenants

Planned water shut-off at Linden Court

Issued 14 December 2026 by Avocet Property Management, Lancaster

Date of worksTuesday 12 January 2027
Supply off between09:00 and 15:00
Buildings affectedBlocks A, B and C
Contact on siteMegan Rahman

We are replacing the main stopcock and the first twelve metres of supply pipe serving Linden Court. To do this safely the mains water to all three blocks must be turned off for the day. We know this is an inconvenience, and we have chosen a weekday outside school holidays so that as few households as possible are affected.

The pipe has been in the ground for more than forty years and has failed twice since the spring. Replacing it now should end the low pressure many of you have reported on the upper floors.

What you need to do

  • Fill a kettle, a few bottles and the bath before 09:00.
  • Turn off the washing machine and dishwasher before you leave.
  • Do not run the hot tap when the supply returns until the pipes have cleared.
  • Tell your neighbours who may not read this notice, especially anyone who is housebound.

Bottled water will be available from the caretaker's office throughout the day. Residents who depend on medical equipment that needs running water should call us by 4 January, and we will arrange a temporary supply.

Questions to Callum Haworth, Estates Manager, on 01524 555 0136 or the Norwich regional office on 01603 555 0171. Thank you for your patience.
