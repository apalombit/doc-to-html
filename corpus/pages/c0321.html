Mill Row: planning submission for the east range

Dear Bethan,

Thank you for your time on site last Thursday. I am writing to confirm the changes we agreed, so that the Trust can approve them before the planning submission goes in at the end of the month. The east range keeps its original brick frontage, and the new roof lights are moved to the rear slope where they will not be seen from the street.

We have also revised the programme. Structural drawings will be issued on 19 February, and the conservation officer will receive the full set the following week. Niamh Ahmed will lead the heritage statement from our Inverness office and has already begun the survey of the old mill race.

I would be grateful if you could return the signed approval form by 12 February. If anything in the enclosed drawings is unclear, please call me directly and we can go through them together.
