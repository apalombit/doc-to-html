how it works

Bring it broken.
Take it home mended.

  1. 1Register at the door. Give your name, the item and what is wrong with it.
  2. 2Take a seat. Tea and biscuits are free while a fixer is found for you.
  3. 3Watch and help. Fixers show you the repair and you do the screwdriver work.
  4. 4Take it home. Last year we mended 7 of every 10 items brought in.
