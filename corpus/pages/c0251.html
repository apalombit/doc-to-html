1.Minutes of the previous meeting

The minutes of February 21, 2023 were approved as circulated, proposed by Trevor Holloway and seconded by Aaliyah Pruitt.

2.Feasibility results

Priya Raman presented the survey and sizing for the clubhouse roof and three carports.

  • Roof survey
    • Usable area is 4,430 ft2 after setbacks
    • Shading study finished; the east carport loses 6% in winter
  • Array sizing
    • 62 panels at 410 W give 25.4 kW of peak power
    • Expected output offsets about 26 t of CO2 a year

Annual output and simple payback were worked out as follows, with a net installed cost of $61,200 and a rate of $0.118 per kWh:

E = P × H × PR = 25.4 kW × 1,650 h × 0.88 ≈ 36,900 kWh
Payback = C ÷ (E × r) = $61,200 ÷ (36,900 × $0.118) ≈ 14.1 years
3.Financing and decisions

Trevor Holloway reported $28,400 in the reserve. The committee agreed to open resident loans for the 1st instalment and to seek two bids for the carport mounts.
