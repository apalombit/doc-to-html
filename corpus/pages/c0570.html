Willow Fen Project · Spring Briefing

Bringing back the playa:
three steps for Lubbock County

  1. 1. Survey every basin that still holds water after rainVolunteers walk 340 basins with a tape and a notebook.
  2. 2. Redirect farm runoff into the lowest ground, not the roadTwelve landowners have already signed easements for the work.
  3. 3. Return native grasses and let the Curlew Trust count birds each AprilLast spring the count logged 1,900 shorebirds on four lakes.

“A dry lake bed is not empty. It is waiting for the next storm, and so are the birds.”

Trevor Hendricks, Santa Fe, NMRemarks to the Southern High Plains water workshop, Lubbock, TX, autumn session.
