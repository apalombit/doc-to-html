Section 4.3

What one batch of bread costs

Every business that makes something must answer a plain question: what did it cost to make one unit? Clover Bakery in Greenville, South Carolina, bakes its sourdough in batches of 1,250 loaves, and the owner needs a cost for each loaf before she can set a price.

She starts by gathering the direct costs of one week’s batch, then adds a share of the overheads that keep the shop open.

Gathering the direct costs

Source: Clover Bakery weekly ledger, Greenville, SC. Overhead is 12% of direct costs.

Dividing the total by 1,250 loaves gives a unit cost of $1.28. Any price below that loses money on every loaf sold.

Key idea. A unit cost is only as good as the costs gathered behind it; leave out the oven and every loaf looks cheaper than it is.
