Seed order closes at the end of the month

By Brooke Gutierrez

Members have until February 28 to put their names against this season's seed order. The co-op buys in bulk from a grower in Greenville, SC, and the savings are passed straight to plots, so every packet ordered through the list is cheaper than the shop.

Last year we ordered 212 packets and wasted none. This year the tomatoes are again the favorite, closely followed by beans, which did well in the cool spring.

“Plan for the garden you will have in July, not the one you see in February.”
