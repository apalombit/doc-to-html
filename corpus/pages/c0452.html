Section 4: Installing and commissioning the CP-3 controller

This section covers wiring, parameter set-up and first start of the CP-3 controller. Work must be done by a competent engineer. Isolate the supply before opening the enclosure.

4.1 Rated data

Supply voltage230 V AC, 50 Hz
Maximum motor current12.5 A
Enclosure ratingIP54
Ambient temperature range-10 to 45 °C
Fuse (slow-blow)16 A

4.3 Commissioning procedure

  1. 1.  Check that all terminals are tightened to the torque in table 4.2 and that the earth lead is connected first.
  2. 2.  Close the enclosure and switch on the supply. The green POWER lamp should light within 3 seconds.
  3. 3.  Hold the MENU key for 5 seconds to enter the parameter list, then set P01 to the motor rated current.
  4. 4.  Set P02 (dry-run delay) to 20 s and P03 (restart delay) to 90 s for a borehole installation.
  5. 5.  Fill the sump to the high-level float and confirm that the pump starts and the RUN lamp lights.
  6. 6.  Record the running current in the commissioning sheet and sign it off with the customer.
Warning. Never bridge the float terminals to force a start. Running the pump dry for more than 30 seconds will damage the seal.
