News from the Sparrow Bluebell Garden Society, Peoria
November 10, 2021
Issue 47

Bulbs are in

Over two crisp weekends in late October, thirty-one members planted just over four thousand bulbs along the creek path. Raj Fitzgerald, who led the work, said the soil was the best it has been since the society began in 1998. Daffodils line the north edge, with bluebells under the old maples.

Lauren Mahoney thanks everyone who brought wheelbarrows, and the Peoria hardware store that gave forty bags of compost at no charge. The first blooms are expected in April, and a walking tour will be arranged once they appear.

Volunteers are still needed to water the new beds on Saturday mornings through the end of the month.

Member notes

Jordan Abernathy has stepped down as treasurer after six years and is thanked for keeping the books in good order. The committee welcomes Lauren Mahoney in the role from January.

Dues for next year remain $25 for individuals and $40 for households. Payment may be made at any meeting or mailed to the society's address in Peoria, IL.

Our sister group in Lexington, KY has invited members to its spring open gardens on May 14 and 15. Carpools will leave from the Peoria library at 8 am.
