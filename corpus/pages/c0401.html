Four routes that earn their September

Soft light, quiet paths and a pub at the end: two towns, two coasts, one season.

Words Ruth Haworth · Photographs Owen Hughes

The crowds have gone home and the bracken has turned. We walked eight routes around Durham and Aberystwyth this summer; these four are the ones we would do again, each with a shorter option for tired legs.
