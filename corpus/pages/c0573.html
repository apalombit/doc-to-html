Limits we measure

  • Graphite anode capacity is near its ceiling at 372 mAh g-1
    • Silicon blends reach 1,200 mAh g-1 but swell by 300%
    • Cycle life falls below 500 cycles above 10% Si
  • Cathode choice sets cost and safety
    • NMC 811: high density, thermal runaway above 210 °C
    • LFP: lower density, stable, no cobalt
  • Pack-level density is about 60% of cell-level
    • Cooling plates and busbars add mass
    • Casing and wiring take a further 15% of pack weight
  • Fast charging shortens life
    • Charging above 2C at 25 °C plates lithium on the anode
    • Cold packs below 0 °C lose about 30% of usable energy
