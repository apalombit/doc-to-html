1. Summary of findings

The unit is a steel-portal warehouse of about 1998 with block infill walls and profiled-sheet cladding. The structure is sound. Defects are mainly weathering of the roof and drainage, together with local damp at the north wall. No evidence of progressive movement was found. Repairs are itemised below with budget costs.
