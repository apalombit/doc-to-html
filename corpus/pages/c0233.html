The inspection covered the low-slope roof, parapets, flashings, drainage and the exterior walls of the three-story building, built in 1998. Access was by roof hatch and from a lift on the north and east sides. Interior ceilings on the top floor were checked where tenants allowed entry.1

A second visit to the sister property in Fresno, CA is scheduled for the fall. Methods: visual survey, moisture meter readings at 40 points, and infrared scan at dusk. Photographs are held in the project file and available on request.

3. Recommendations

Immediate. Clear the two blocked drains and re-seal the open seams along the north slope before the summer storm season. Ponding up to 1.5 in. was measured near drain D4.3

Within 12 months. Replace failed sealant at the east windows, re-bed the parapet coping and repair the blistered patches. Re-inspect after repairs and report to the owner.
