Frequently Asked Questions

Nuthatch Vale Public Library, Fort Collins, Colorado · updated February 27, 2022
  1. 1. How do I get a library card?Bring a photo ID and proof of address to any desk. Residents of Fort Collins and Bangor, ME, students and staff of local schools may apply. Cards are issued the same day.
  2. 2. How many items can I borrow?Up to 30 items at once, of which no more than 5 may be DVDs and no more than 3 may be new releases.
  3. 3. How long can I keep them?Books and audiobooks go out for three weeks; DVDs and tools for one week. See the table below for details.
    ItemLoanFine/day
    Books21 days$0.10
    DVDs7 days$0.25
    Tools7 days$0.50
  4. 4. Can I renew online?Yes. Sign in with your card number and PIN, choose “My account” and press Renew. Items with a hold on them cannot be renewed.
  5. 5. What if I lose something?Tell us as soon as you can. You will be charged the replacement price, and the charge is cancelled if the item turns up within 60 days.
  6. 6. Is there a charge for printing?Black and white pages are $0.15 each, color pages $0.50. The first ten pages each day are free for cardholders.
  7. 7. Can I book a meeting room?Rooms for 4 to 12 people can be booked up to 30 days ahead from the front desk. Ask Kayla Holloway, our rooms coordinator, for the form.
  8. 8. Do you run events for children?Story time is held on Tuesdays and Saturdays at 10:30 a.m. Brandon Abernathy leads the Saturday sessions.
  9. 9. Where do I send suggestions?Write to the Library Director, Sofia Brennan, or leave a note in the box by the door.
