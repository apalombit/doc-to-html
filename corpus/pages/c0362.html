4.3 Running costs

The ST-90 burns between 1.2 and 3.8 pounds of pellets an hour, depending on the setting. At the average price paid by owners in Wichita, KS, a 40 lb bag costs $7.50, so the fuel cost of a heating day follows directly from the dial.1

Figure 4.2   Fuel cost per 12-hour day: $2.65, $4.45, $6.50 and $8.40 at settings 1 to 4.
Note. Prices vary. Multiply the pounds burned per hour by your own price per pound.2

Seasonal estimate

An owner who heats for 150 days at setting 2 should expect about $667.50 in pellets. Moving to setting 3 raises that to $975.00.

1 Based on pellets of 8,000 BTU per pound.
2 Bulk pallet prices are usually lower, near $5.90 per 40 lb bag.
