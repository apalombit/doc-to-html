5. Findings and approval
Report JMT-26-1187 · issued 10 December 2026

Findings

Twelve 100 mm cubes were cast from the C32/40 mix at the Acorn Yard site, Truro, on 12 November 2026 and crushed after 28 days of water curing at 20 °C.

  • Mean compressive strength 46.8 N/mm2; the characteristic strength estimate1 is 41.3 N/mm2.
  • Individual results run from 44.1 to 49.6 N/mm2; no cube fell below 40 N/mm2.
  • Mean fresh density 2,395 kg/m3, within 1.5 % of the declared 2,420 kg/m3.
  • Eleven cubes failed in the satisfactory cone pattern; one failed in columnar shear.

Method and limits

Cubes were tested to procedure JMT-C-04 in the 2,000 kN frame at Derby, loaded at 0.6 N/mm2 per second. The frame was last calibrated on 14/09/2026; expanded uncertainty ±1.2 %.

1 Mean of the twelve results less 1.64 times the standard deviation (2.8 N/mm2).
