Where the studio worked, and what each commission taught us.

Ardnamurchan lodge

A timber retreat on a windswept peninsula, built from larch felled within ten miles.

Salford wharf housing

Sixty homes in a former cotton warehouse, with the original cast-iron frame left bare.

Whitby bakery

A small shop front restored to its 1920s tiling, with a new oven room behind.

Kew garden pavilion

A glazed pavilion in the herb garden, open on three sides to the weather.
