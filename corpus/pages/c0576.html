Fresno’s nuthatches are thinning out while Mobile’s hold steady

Indext = 100 × (Vt ÷ Ht) ÷ (V2015 ÷ H2015)

V = nuthatch visits logged, H = observer-hours at the feeder, t = season

Figure 1. Raw rates in Fresno fell from 3.1 visits an hour in 2015 to 2.2 this summer, while Mobile moved between 4.0 and 4.5. Volunteer Mei Nguyen, who has logged the Fresno yard since the first season, puts the drop down to the loss of two old oaks on the next block.

Source: Dovetail Bird Count, volunteer logs, Fresno, CA and Mobile, AL. Data retrieved August 16, 2019. Chart by Sofia Fitzgerald.
