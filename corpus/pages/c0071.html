Claim for Business Asset Disposal Relief (known as Entrepreneurs’ Relief before 6 April 2020)
– section 169M TCGA 1992

When you make a claim as an individual, this will normally be done at the time you make your tax return. If however,
you’re unable to make a claim in a return, you may do this by completing Section A of this form. If a claim is made by
the trustees of a settlement, then it must be made jointly by both the trustees and the qualifying beneficiary. To make the
claim using this form the qualifying beneficiary should complete Section A and the trustee of the settlement, Section B.
