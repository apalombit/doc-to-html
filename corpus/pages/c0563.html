Questions we hear most

Can I switch plans?Yes. Upgrades apply at once and downgrades start at your next bill, with unused days credited.
Do you offer discounts?Schools, community gardens and food banks pay 50% on any plan. Write to Rachel Nguyen to apply.
Where is my data kept?On servers in Duluth, MN and Provo, UT, copied nightly. You can export everything at any time.
Is there a contract?No. Plans renew monthly and you can cancel from your account page in two clicks.
Do you help us move over?Send us your spreadsheets and we load them for you, free on Orchard and Canopy.
What if a frost alert is wrong?Tell us the plot and the date and we credit that month in full.
