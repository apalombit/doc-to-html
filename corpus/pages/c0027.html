Before you begin

  1. A. Gather your papers Required
    • Wage statements (Form W-2 or equivalent)
    • Interest and dividend notices
    • Prior-year return, if you filed one
  2. B. Complete in this order Required
    • Part 1, filer details
      • Use your legal name
      • Print one letter per box
    • Part 2, household members
    • Part 3, income and credits
  3. C. Attach if they apply Optional
    • Schedule R, rental income
    • Statement of dependent care costs
