Tansy Quarter Walk, Kendal
Self-guided route of 1.4 miles, about 50 minutes at an easy pace
River Kent Highfield Rd Mill Street Puffin Lane Tansy Gardens Castle Green Market Hall Station Rd Railway Bridge St 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 N 0 100 200 m
No. PlaceWhat to look for
Route stops
1 Mill Bridge Packhorse bridge over the Kent; start here
2 Bridge Street arch Weir and fish ladder, best seen at the rail
3 Tansy Gardens Walled garden, planted by the 1920 gardeners' guild
Facilities
4 Market Hall café Open 09:00 to 16:30; toilets and baby change
5 Station Road car park 42 spaces, pay and display, free on Sundays
6 Puffin Lane kiosk Information point; free printed maps in the rack
Viewpoint
7 Castle Green Ruined tower and a view of the whole town
Key: red dashed line = walking route; brown dashes = rail line. Surveyed on 27/01/2020 by Declan Mensah; checked by Bethan Mercer and Arjun Burrows. Route leaflets are available from the Lincoln office of the trail group.
