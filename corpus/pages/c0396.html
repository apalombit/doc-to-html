4Setting a schedule

The CX-8 runs up to three independent programs, labeled A, B and C. Each program has its own start times, run times and watering days. Most lawns need one program; use a second for drip beds and a third for newly seeded areas.

4.1Choose a program

  • Turn the dial to SCHEDULE. The display shows the active program letter.
  • Press PROG to move between A, B and C.
    • Program A is on at the factory; B and C are off until you give them a start time.
    • A program that is off keeps its settings but never runs.
  • Press DAYS and select the days to water:
    • Specific weekdays, such as Tuesday and Saturday.
    • Odd or even calendar dates, for cities with date-based limits.
    • An interval of every 2 to 14 days.

4.2Set start times

Each program accepts up to four start times a day. Every start time runs all the zones in that program, one after another, so one entry is usually enough.

  • Press START and use the arrow keys to set the hour and minute.
  • Press ADD for a second start time, for example 5:30 am and again at 9:00 pm.
  • To remove one, set it to OFF by pressing both arrow keys together.
Caution
Do not schedule start times between 10:00 am and 4:00 pm in summer. Water lost to evaporation can reach 30 percent, and wet leaves in full sun may scorch.

4.3Set run times

  • Turn the dial to RUN TIME and press ZONE to select a zone from 1 to 8.
  • Enter minutes from 1 to 240. Typical settings are:
    • Lawn with spray heads: 12 to 15 minutes.
    • Lawn with rotors: 25 to 40 minutes.
    • Drip lines and shrub beds: 45 to 60 minutes.
  • Repeat for every zone that the program should water. A zone left at 0 is skipped.

4.4Seasonal adjustment

Press % to scale every run time at once, from 10 to 200 percent in steps of 10. Raise it in July and lower it in October instead of editing each zone.

Tip. Write your final settings on the label inside the cabinet door before you close it.
