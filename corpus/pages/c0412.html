6    Full-Cohort Public Reanalysis

We separately evaluate a frozen list of 1,047 unique
expressions selected from 3,114 preserved input
rows using the stated IS screen. This does not es-
tablish a census of all generated candidates or a
historically blind holdout. The broader preserved
pool is not a matched replay of the three historical
keep totals. All IDs are retained regardless of sub-
sequent public-evaluator outcome; no expression
is chosen by its new score.

The new snapshot contains the same ordered
40-stock convenience universe over 727 trading
dates in 2023–2025. We acquire both unadjusted
and provider-adjusted daily views through BaoS-
tock 0.9.3 and verify all 80 files, their byte hashes,

date/stock/adjustment identities, and shared quan-
tity and trading-status fields. Features use nomi-
nal prices, shares, and nominal traded amount; the
adjusted-price view is reserved for labels. Two
non-trading stock-day cells are masked. We neither
forward-fill missing observations nor substitute a
smaller acquired stock prefix.
For each 2024–2025 date t, the label is the
provider-adjusted close change over five trading-
calendar rows,

yi,t = Padji,t+5Padji,t − 1.

Both endpoint prices must be finite and positive
and both endpoints tradable. This is not an investor
total-return or executable-P&L claim; the last five
captured dates have no label. Daily rank-IC is the
Pearson correlation of average-tie cross-sectional
ranks. We require at least five eligible stocks, three
distinct signal values, and two distinct labels. The
three-signal-value threshold is a chosen diagnostic
convention, not a claim that binary signals cannot
have a mathematically defined correlation. A factor
is scorable only with at least 20 valid dates. Its
mean/SD ratio, if reported, is unannualized and is
not a trading Sharpe.

The frozen public implementation initially pro-
duces 57 numerical errors: its conditional operator
attempts to interpret a panel as one Boolean when
the true branch is scalar. We preserve this v1 run
and add a versioned scalar-broadcasting correction
with the existing elementwise conditional seman-
tics. All 1,047 records are then re-evaluated, rather
than rerunning only promising failures. The other
990 diagnostic records remain unchanged; 56 cor-
rected records become scorable and one remains
insufficiently observed.
