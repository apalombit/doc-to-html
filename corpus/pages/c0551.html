Seven Days on the Cornish Coast Path

A guided, unhurried week between Padstow and Land's End, with a bed, a hot supper and a lift for your bags each night.

Small groups of 8Moderate gradeDeparts Saturdays

How the week runs

  1. 1Arrive in Padstow, meet your guide and walk the estuary to Harlyn Bay.
  2. 2Cliff path to Porthcothan; a long day, with a pub lunch at Mawgan Porth.
  3. 3Newquay to Perranporth along the dunes, tide permitting.
  4. 4St Ives and the Zennor cliffs, the wildest stretch of the walk.
  5. 5Rest day in Penzance, or an optional boat to the Isles of Scilly.

Good to know

Walking days run four to six hours.
Total ascent about 480 metres a day.
Six nights in family-run guesthouses.
