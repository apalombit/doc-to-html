Housing

Rent freeze vote passes

Members of the Eastfield Tenants Union voted 212 to 41 on October 3 to ask the landlord association for a twelve month freeze on rents across all seven buildings on Alder Street and Corbett Row. The motion was moved by the Corbett Row committee, who pointed to three consecutive increases since 2024.

The association has promised a written reply by November 15. Until then, members are asked not to sign new lease terms without first speaking to a union steward at the Thursday clinic.

Report by Ines Calderon
Repairs

Boiler fixes reach block C

After eleven weeks of complaints the managing agent has finally replaced the shared boiler in block C. Residents of the forty flats on floors one to five report that radiators were warm again from October 9, and hot water pressure is back to normal on the upper floors.

Blocks A and B are next on the schedule. If your flat still has a cold radiator, log it using the repairs sheet in the lobby and add the date. A written record of each fault matters when we escalate a case to the council.

Report by Walt Hanrahan
Events

Autumn harvest supper

Our yearly supper returns to the community hall on Saturday, November 7, from six in the evening. The kitchen team is cooking squash soup, three kinds of bread and a large tray of baked apples, with vegetarian and gluten free dishes at every table.

Tickets are four dollars for adults and free for children under twelve. Volunteers are needed for setting up from three o'clock and for washing up afterwards. Please sign the sheet on the noticeboard by Friday, October 30.

Anyone interested can put their name forward at the Thursday clinic or by writing to the secretary at the address printed below. Nominations close on November 20.

Housing

Know your notice rights

A landlord must give written notice before entering a flat, normally twenty four hours, except in an emergency such as a burst pipe. Several members reported unannounced visits by contractors in September, and the union has raised the matter with the agent.

If this happens to you, note the date, the time and who came, and tell your steward the same week. A short factual note is the most useful thing you can hand us.

Guide by Mara Oyelaran

Winter coat swap

Bring a clean coat you no longer wear and take home one that fits. The swap runs in the hall on Sunday, November 15, between ten in the morning and two in the afternoon. Children's sizes are always the first to go, so please come early if you are looking for one.

Unclaimed coats will be given to the shelter on Marsh Road at the end of the day, with the thanks of the whole union.
