Atlas Freight Group · Board report, Q4 2020

5Operational risk and Board decisions

Position at 19 February 2021, as recorded at the Board meeting held at the Carlisle depot

  1. 5.1The Board reviewed the risk register prepared by Leah Doherty, Head of Risk, and agreed the residual ratings shown in Table 5.
  2. 5.2Carlisle depot. The Board noted that:
    1. (a)the replacement of the cold-store compressor was completed on 8 January 2021, six days behind schedule;
    2. (b)the annual insurer inspection took place on 2 February 2021 and raised no major findings.

  1. 5.3Inverness hub. Imogen Mensah, Chief Operating Officer, reported that the second sorting line now carries 62% of parcels and that peak-week clearance has improved from 31 to 26 hours.
  2. 5.4The Board resolved to:
    1. (a)approve a further 14 driver posts across Carlisle and Inverness, to be filled by 30 June 2021;
