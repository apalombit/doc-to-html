Onboarding, part two

Setting up a new site visit

Four steps take a field team from booking to a signed report. Follow them in order and the survey is ready within a day.

Priya Haworth, Lancaster
Presented 19 January 2021
Slide 4 of 12
  1. 1Book the visit. Choose a date with Aisha Odell and confirm access with the owner.
  2. 2Pack the kit. Take the survey tablet, laser tape, camera, torch and two spare batteries.
  3. 3Walk the site. Record each room in the app and photograph every defect found.
  4. 4Send the report. Imogen Stirling checks it and returns it to the client in Salisbury.
