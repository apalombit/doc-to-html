Minutes of the Committee

Meeting held by video call on Friday 12 June 2020 at 7.00 p.m.

Present: Zara Treloar (Chair), Sophie Mercer (Treasurer, joining from Bangor), Niamh Bryce (Secretary). The minutes of the meeting of 8 May 2020 were approved without amendment.

5.Water charges for 2020/21

The Treasurer presented the four quarterly invoices from the water supplier for the year to 31 March 2020, together with the annual standing charge of £186.00. She noted that the Society now has 48 plots in use, and proposed that the cost be divided equally among them, as in previous years, after rounding to the nearest pound.

Table 1. Metered water charges, April 2019 to March 2020

Metered total, M1,284.00

With M the metered total, S the standing charge and N the number of plots, the charge per plot is found from

C = (M + S) ÷ N = (1,284.00 + 186.00) ÷ 48 = £30.63

The committee discussed whether plot-holders who share a standpipe should pay less. It was felt that the saving would be small and the bookkeeping disproportionate.

Resolved: that the water charge for 2020/21 be set at £31.00 per plot, payable with the annual rent by 30 September.
