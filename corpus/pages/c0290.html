Minutes · Trustees’ meeting

Tern Community Trust

Thursday 28 May 2026, 6.30 pm · Pebble Street Hall, Aberystwyth

Present: Niamh Lindsay (chair), Callum Rees (treasurer), Bethan Patel (secretary). Apologies were received from two trustees. The minutes of the last meeting were agreed and signed.

3.Hall refurbishment

Callum Rees reported that the surveyor’s quotation for the roof and the new kitchen came to £48,600 including VAT, against the £42,000 the trust has set aside. Niamh Lindsay asked whether the Hereford grant fund would meet the difference.

“We should not sign anything until the survey is back and we know what is under the floor.”Callum Rees

After discussion the trustees agreed:

  1. 1.to accept the quotation in principle, subject to the survey;
  2. 2.to ask the Hereford grant fund for £6,600 towards the shortfall;
  3. 3.to hold the kitchen works until the roof is finished.

4.Actions

  • Bethan Patel to send the revised budget to all trustees by 5 June.
  • Callum Rees to submit the grant application by 19 June.
  • Niamh Lindsay to ask the surveyor for a date to lift the floor.
  • All trustees to return the key-holder form.

5.Any other business

Bethan Patel reported that the Tuesday toddler group has asked to move to Wednesday mornings. Agreed, from the start of June. The meeting closed at 8.05 pm.
