2. About the study

You are invited to take part in a 12-week study of how sleep patterns relate to blood pressure and heart rhythm. Taking part is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without any effect on your usual care. By signing below you confirm that:

  • the study has been explained to you and you have had time to ask questions;
  • you understand the possible risks, including mild skin irritation from the sensor patches;
  • you understand that your data will be stored under your participant ID and not your name;
  • you agree to be contacted by the study team about follow-up visits.

4. Your rights

Your data is kept for ten years in a locked database at the Unit and is seen only by authorised staff and the ethics committee. Results are published as group findings; nobody will be identifiable. If you are harmed as a direct result of the study, the sponsor will cover reasonable treatment costs.
