5.Delivery

5.1Keystone Aggregates, Inc. (“Supplier”) shall deliver the Materials to the Duluth, MN yard of Quarry Hill Builders LLC (“Buyer”) on the dates set out in Schedule B.

5.2Every delivery arrives with a weigh ticket that shows:

  • gross, tare and net weight in short tons;
  • the product grade and the quarry of origin;
  • the truck number and the time of loading.

5.3Risk of loss passes to Buyer when the load is tipped at the yard and the ticket is signed by Buyer’s yard foreman.

6.Quality

6.1The Materials shall meet the gradation limits in Schedule A. Buyer may reject a load by written notice within 48 hours of delivery, stating:

(a)the weigh ticket number;

(b)the defect found, with photographs where the defect is visible; and

(c)whether the load is held or returned.

6.2Supplier shall replace a rejected load within five business days at no charge to Buyer.

7.Price and payment

7.1The price is $18.40 per short ton delivered, plus a fuel surcharge that shall not exceed 4 percent of the price in any month.

7.2Supplier invoices Buyer on the last day of each month. Buyer shall pay within 30 days of the invoice date, by check or by ACH transfer to the account named on the invoice.

7.3Amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1.5 percent per month until paid.

8.Term and termination

8.1This Agreement takes effect on February 2, 2025 and runs for 24 months.

8.2Either party may end it early on 60 days’ written notice if:

  • the other party fails to cure a material breach within 30 days of notice; or
  • the other party becomes insolvent or ceases to trade.

8.3Termination does not release either party from sums already due.
