3. Commissioning procedure

  1. 3.1  Isolate the supply at the board and prove it dead.
  2. 3.2  Fit the probe cables to X3, screen to the earth bar only.
  3. 3.3  Restore the supply; the display shows the firmware version for 5 s.
  4. 3.4  Enter the setting menu and record start, stop and alarm levels above.
  5. 3.5  Raise the sump level by hand; the pump relay must close at the start level.
WARNING. Mains voltage is present on X1 and X2 whenever the cabinet is live. Work only on a locked-off supply.
