Master Services Agreement

Warehouse scheduling and dispatch support

This Master Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on October 28, 2020, between the parties named below, who agree as follows.

Provider: Northgate Freight Systems, Inc., a Utah corporation with its offices in Provo, UT, represented by Camila Kowalczyk, Chief Operating Officer.

Client: Atlas Print Works, LLC, a Georgia company with its offices in Macon, GA, represented by Kayla Fitzgerald, Director of Operations.

1.Definitions

1.1“Services” means the scheduling, dispatch and reporting work described in Section 2 and in any order form signed by both parties.

1.2“Deliverables” means the written reports, route plans and data files that the Provider hands over to the Client under this Agreement.

1.3“Confidential Information” means any non-public information that one party discloses to the other, in any form, and marks or reasonably treats as confidential.

2.Scope of Services

2.1The Provider will perform the Services with reasonable care and skill, and will assign a named account manager to the Client for the whole term.

2.2The Services include the following, each as set out in the order form:

  • weekly route planning for the Client’s Macon loading dock;
  • daily dispatch confirmations sent before 6:00 a.m. Eastern time;
  • a monthly report on late deliveries, load factors and fuel use.

3.Fees and Payment

3.1The Client will pay the Provider a monthly fee of $4,800.00, plus any sales tax that applies. Invoices follow these steps:

  1. (1)The Provider issues an invoice on the last business day of each month.
  2. (2)The Client pays the invoice within thirty (30) days of receiving it.
  3. (3)An amount not paid when due bears interest at 1.5% per month until it is paid.

3.2Fees are reviewed once a year, and any change takes effect only if both parties sign it in writing.

4.Term and Confidentiality

4.1This Agreement starts on November 1, 2020 and runs for twenty-four (24) months. Either party may end it by giving ninety (90) days’ written notice.

4.2Each party will keep the other’s Confidential Information secret, use it only for this Agreement, and return or destroy it when the Agreement ends.

4.3This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Utah.
