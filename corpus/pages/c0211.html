Project Status Report: Riverside Depot Refit

Reporting period: 1 to 21 November 2025 · Issued 21 November 2025 · Report no. 11

1. Summary

The refit of the Riverside depot remains eleven working days behind the programme agreed in September. The structural steelwork is complete and signed off. The roof covering, which governs everything that follows, was held up by three weeks of high winds. The budget stands at 4.2% above the approved figure, almost wholly due to the cost of the extended scaffold hire.

2. Progress this period

  1. 1. Structure and envelope
    • (a) Steelwork inspected by Yusuf Chowdhury and accepted on 6 November.
    • (b) Roof sheets fixed to bays 1 to 5; bays 6 to 8 awaiting dry weather.
  2. 2. Services
    • (a) First-fix electrical complete in the office block.
    • (b) Heating pipework pressure-tested without loss.
    • (c) Fire alarm panel delivered from the Swindon warehouse.
  3. 3. Finishes
    • (a) Floor screed poured in the workshop on 17 November; curing for 14 days.
