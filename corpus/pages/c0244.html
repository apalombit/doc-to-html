1.Summary

Overall status is amber. Field work on the Northgate corridor is 61% complete against a plan of 64%, and cumulative spend stands at $3,705,000 against a planned $3,640,000, a variance of +$65,000 (+1.8%).1 The three-week slip in pipe deliveries from the Tulsa, OK fabrication yard is the main driver. It has been absorbed on the critical path by resequencing the Segment C bores, and the 3rd Street crossing remains the controlling activity. No lost-time incidents were recorded in the period; the project stands at 118 days without one.

5.Risks and open issues

  • Pipe delivery. Tulsa yard output is 14 joints a week against 20 planned.
    • Second shift approved by the yard from July 6; effect not yet confirmed.
    • Fallback is 640 ft of ductile-iron spools for the 3rd Street crossing, at +$38,000.
  • Groundwater in Segment B. Dewatering continues at 85 gal/min.
    • Permit discharge limit is 120 gal/min; the margin is adequate through the July 4th week.
    • Turbidity readings are logged daily and sit below 25 NTU.
  • Utility conflicts at Bore 2. A gas main is 2.1 ft shallower than the potholing survey showed.
    • Designer’s revised profile received June 22 and under owner review.
    • A 1st-priority decision is requested from Mr. Tillman by July 2.
  • Weather. Four rain days lost in June; the contract allows ten before a time extension.
