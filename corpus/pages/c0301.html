This notice tells you that the rent for your unit will be reviewed under the Authority's annual schedule. No change takes effect before thirty (30) days from the date above, and any change will be confirmed to you in writing.

What happens next

  • A review officer, Tyler Tillman, will check your household record.
    • Bring proof of income received in the last three months.
    • Tell us of any change in who lives in the unit.
  • You may ask for a meeting at the Tacoma office or by telephone.
    • Requests must reach us within fourteen (14) days.
  • A written decision follows within thirty (30) days.

If you disagree with the decision you may ask for an informal hearing before a different officer. Tenants who live in the Flagstaff, AZ properties managed by this Authority should use the form supplied with their local notice.
