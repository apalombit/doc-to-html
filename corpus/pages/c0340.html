Following last week’s call, this memo fixes the terms of the transfer so that both branches can plan their docks. Please read each clause and reply by November 12.

1.   Schedule

1.1Duluth will ship 40 pallets of dock hardware and 12 pallets of winterizing kits to Erie between November 16 and December 4, in six loads.

1.2Erie will keep the receiving dock open Monday through Thursday, 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., with a forklift operator on every delivery day.

1.3Tyler Gutierrez will check each pick list against the bill of lading before a truck leaves Duluth, and send the count to both branches the same day.

2.   Costs

2.1Freight is charged to Duluth’s transfer account at $1,840.00 per load, $11,040.00 in all.

2.2Any damaged goods found at Erie are reported within 48 hours, with photographs, so that the carrier claim can be filed.
