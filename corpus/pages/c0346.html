Our offices stay closed through May

Both offices, in Bangor and in Erie, stay closed to staff until Monday, June 1, 2020. The guidance we are following has not changed since our last note, and the order covering Maine runs to the same date.1 Nobody is asked to travel to either site before then, and the reception desks will take no visitors.

Everyone who can do their work from home should keep doing so. Darnell Le has raised the VPN capacity to 400 connections, up from 250, and the help desk is open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Eastern. If your home set-up needs a monitor or a chair, Luis Ramirez will arrange a loan from the stock held in the Bangor store room.2

Staff whose work needs the labs, the print room or the mail desk should write to me by Friday, May 8. We will keep a short list of approved visits, each with a named host, and share the rules in the week of May 18.

Thank you for the care you are taking of each other.
