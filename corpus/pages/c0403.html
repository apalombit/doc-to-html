Question two

How do I make a booking?

  1. 1.Look at the diary in the entrance or ask the office which dates are free.
  2. 2.Fill in the booking form and return it with a deposit of £50.00.
  3. 3.Wait for our confirmation letter, which arrives within five working days.
  4. 4.Pay the balance 14 days before your event.

Summer bookings open on Monday 2 March 2026.

Question three

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel at least 28 days before your event and the deposit is returned in full. Inside 28 days we keep it. Inside seven days the whole hire charge is due.

Your date goes back into the diary at once, so please tell Ellie Rees at the office as soon as you know.

Question four

Is there parking and step-free access?

The car park off Birch Lane holds 24 cars, with two bays kept for blue badge holders.

The main hall is step-free from the lane door, and a hearing loop covers the stage end. The Birch Room is on the first floor with a lift.

Still stuck? Ask Amelia Caldwell, bookings secretary, or Zara Sinclair, caretaker, on 01632 960417.
