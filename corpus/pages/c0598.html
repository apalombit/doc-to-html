Billing questions

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Upgrades apply at once and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

What counts as a member?

Anyone who can create or edit work. Guests who only read and comment are always free.

Do you offer discounts?

Registered non-profits and schools receive 30% off Team and Business plans on request.

How do I pay?

By card, or by invoice on annual Business and Enterprise contracts, with net 30 terms.
