The WW-240 measures volumetric water content and temperature at depths to 60 cm. It is built for orchards, turf and research plots and runs for two seasons on one lithium cell.1

Output is a 0–2.5 V analog signal or an SDI-12 reading. A stainless 8 mm rod and an epoxy-sealed head resist frost and fertilizer salts.
