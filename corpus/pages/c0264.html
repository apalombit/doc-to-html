Hospital course

Mrs Zara Mercer, 64, was admitted through the emergency department on 21/01/2027 with a four-day history of productive cough, fever and pleuritic chest pain. A chest X-ray showed right lower lobe consolidation and her CURB-65 score was 2. She was given intravenous co-amoxiclav for 48 hours and then changed to oral antibiotics. Oxygen was weaned off by day 3 and her CRP fell from 214 to 38 mg/L. Creatinine rose to 118 µmol/L on admission and ramipril was reduced. She was mobilising independently on 27/01/2027.
