Merlin Lantern Cottages

Three restored farm cottages a short drive from Ipswich, and a fourth by the water outside Norwich. Wood stoves, deep baths and nobody else for a mile.

Each cottage sleeps four to six, with a fully fitted kitchen, linen and towels provided, and a basket of logs by the door. Dogs are welcome in two of them. Ewan Mensah, who keeps the farm, leaves fresh eggs and a bottle of cider for every new arrival.
